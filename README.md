# MedCore HMS — Enterprise Hospital Management & Clinical EMR

MedCore HMS is an enterprise-grade, human-crafted clinical operations platform designed for hospitals, outpatient polyclinics, and multi-facility healthcare networks.

### Core Capabilities:
- **Outpatient Scheduling Desk**: 3-way view switcher (List, Interactive Month Calendar with Day Inspector, and Physician Roster Board) with real-time double-booking conflict detection.
- **Multi-Tenant & 9-Role RBAC**: Dedicated workspaces for Super Admin, Hospital Admin, Attending Physicians, Triage Nurses, Receptionists, Lab Technicians, Pharmacists, Billing Accountants, and Patients.
- **Inpatient Ward & Bed Censuses**: Floorplan bed allocation with real-time occupancy and sanitization tracking.
- **Clinical SOAP Consultation**: Digital vitals intake, ICD-10 diagnostic coding, and drug-drug safety cross-referencing.
- **Pharmacy & Dispensary**: Expiry-indexed FIFO inventory management with automated batch deduction.
- **Revenue Cycle & TPA Billing**: Multi-item consolidated invoices with automated insurance adjudication.
- **Compliance & Security**: Normalized 3NF relational data model, immutable HIPAA audit logging, and responsive viewport support across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
